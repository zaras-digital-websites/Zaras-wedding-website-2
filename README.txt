SHIFANA & ZABEEL — WEDDING INVITATION DEMO

HOW TO VIEW IN GOOGLE CHROME
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the folder named shifana-zabeel-wedding.
3. Double-click index.html (or right-click > Open with > Google Chrome).
4. Click “Open Invitation” to enter the animated invitation.

The website is self-contained apart from Google Fonts, which require an internet connection. The uploaded couple photo is included in assets/couple-photo.png.

Included sections: opening invitation, animated countdown, bride and groom family details, Nikah ceremony and venue link, invitation message, best wishes, and footer.
